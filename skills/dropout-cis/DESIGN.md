# DESIGN.md — DropOut Tech (琢奧科技)

## Visual Thesis
Light-first enterprise engineering aesthetic: **80% Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) + 20% Ice Mist (`#EEF8F7`)**, anchored by **Abyss Navy (`#153D61`)**, **DropOut Teal (`#3CA59E`)**, and **Action Amber (`#F09336`)** as the single primary conversion signal. Designed for zero-friction scannability by time-poor, ADHD-prone business owners.

## Typography
- **Chinese**: `Noto Sans TC`
- **Latin & Numerals**: `Archivo` (headings/KPIs) + `Inter` (UI/body), with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on all prices and metrics.

## Layout & Cognitive Safety
- 8px spatial grid (`80–96px` section vertical rhythm on desktop, `56px` on mobile).
- Max 2 CTAs in Hero; zero auto-advancing carousels or tickers; zero overlapping floating widgets.
- 48px uniform height for inputs and `<select>` controls with custom right-aligned chevrons.

## Anti-AI-Slop Visuals
- No generic stock icons in rounded-square boxes.
- Use real Micro-UI snippets (sparklines, workflow status pills, live voice status bars) and custom DropOut-DNA SVG diagrams (3-layer isometric stack, intersecting `dO` closed-loop verification).
